## The problem

The client needed a launch page that felt like a place, not a page, and it
had to load fast on a mid-range phone.

## Process

- Blocked out the city in Blender, then rebuilt it procedurally in Rust.
- Baked lighting for the static districts; kept neon dynamic.
- Streamed districts in as the camera approached them.
